Straight answers

No. Your customers keep calling the number they already know. The text back is set up around it.

No. They're short, friendly, and written in your business's voice. You read and approve the wording before anything goes live, and you can change it whenever you like.

The AI asks how urgent it is. If it's a no-heat, no-cool, or active leak situation, you get flagged right away so you can call them back first.

Not always. The audit looks at what you have. If your site already gets calls, we keep it and add the follow-up. If it's costing you jobs, I'll show you why.
